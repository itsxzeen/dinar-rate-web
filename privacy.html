Privacy Policy
Last updated August 2026

What this site does

Se3rForIQD shows live Iraqi dinar exchange rates, gold and silver prices, and a currency converter. No account is required to use it, and we don't collect your name, email, or identity just to browse rates.

What's stored on your device

  • Your language and default city preference (local storage only)
  • A cached copy of the last rates fetched, so the page has something to show if you're briefly offline

None of this leaves your device unless stated below.

Push notifications

If you use the Se3rForIQD iOS app and turn on Rate Alerts, your device's push token is sent to our server so we can notify you of rate changes. Turning the toggle off removes your token from our server. The website does not use push notifications.

Ads

This site or app may load Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies or device identifiers as described in their own privacy policy.

Third parties

Rate and price data is pulled from our own backend, which in turn sources figures from public exchange and gold-market feeds. We don't sell or share your data with data brokers.
