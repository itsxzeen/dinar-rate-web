Market Rates

The live rate for one city, straight from whichever source channel posted it most recently. Green dot = fresh, red = stale.

Rate Control

Manually pin a city's rate — it overrides live source data until you clear it or set a new one. "Reset to live" clears every override at once.

Rate Sources

Any number of channels can be active at once. For each city, whichever active channel posted most recently wins — including your manual overrides above, which stay in effect until a channel posts something newer. Tap a channel to see which cities it covers and how many times each rate has changed.
