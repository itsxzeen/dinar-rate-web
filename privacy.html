Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

What Se3rForIQD does

Se3rForIQD shows live Iraqi dinar exchange rates, gold and silver prices, nearby exchange shops, and a converter for currency and metals. Viewing rates on the website or in the iOS/Android apps does not require an account.

Your account

Creating an account in the apps is optional. If you create one, we collect your first and last name, email address, phone number, and password. Passwords are stored as salted hashes; we do not store the plain password.

Your phone number may be verified with a one-time code sent by OTPIQ through SMS or WhatsApp. We share the number with OTPIQ only to send that verification code. Account details are used for app functionality such as sign-in, password reset, profile, sync, notification settings, and account deletion. They are not sold and are not used for advertising.

What's stored on your device

  • Your language and default city preference (local storage only)
  • A cached copy of the last rates fetched, so the app or website has something to show if you're briefly offline
  • Widget and display preferences in the iOS app when you configure widgets

None of this leaves your device unless stated below.

Push notifications

If you use the apps and allow notifications, your device push token is sent to our server so we can notify you of rate, gold, or silver changes. Turning notifications off in your system settings stops future notifications. The website does not use browser push notifications.

Location

The Map feature asks for your location only to search for currency exchange shops near you or near the place you moved the map to. Your location is used for that search, is not stored on our servers, and is not shared with advertisers or data brokers. You can deny or revoke location access; the rest of the app still works without it.

Feedback and contact

If you submit the feedback/contact form, we receive the message you type plus the name, phone number, or email address you choose to include. We use that information to read, manage, and respond to your feedback.

Usage analytics

The apps may record anonymous usage information such as an app-generated random ID, which pages are visited, and whether you are using the app or website. This helps us understand whether Se3rForIQD is working and which parts need improvement. This analytics ID is not linked to your account identity.

Ads

The website may load Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies or device identifiers according to Google's own privacy policy and controls.

Third parties

Rate and price data is pulled from our backend, which sources figures from public exchange and gold-market feeds. The backend runs on Cloudflare. Phone verification runs through OTPIQ. We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.

Tracking

The iOS privacy manifest declares that Se3rForIQD does not use collected data for cross-app tracking.

Your choices

  • You can use the main rate, gold, converter, and map features without creating an account.
  • You can remove app permissions such as location or notifications in system settings.
  • You can request account or data help through the Contact page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data — email info@xzeenwaves.us.
